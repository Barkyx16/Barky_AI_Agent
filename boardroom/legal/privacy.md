# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: {updated}_

This policy explains what {company} collects through {product}, and why.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
- **Your meetings:** the questions and background you submit, the advisors' remarks, verdicts, plans and your progress.
- **Billing:** if you subscribe, our payment processor (Stripe) handles your card details. We receive only a customer ID and your subscription status.
- **Technical data:** basic request data such as IP addresses, used for security and rate limiting.

## How we use it

To run the service: generate your meetings, keep your history, send the emails you ask for (password resets, review reminders), process payments, prevent abuse, and understand usage and cost in aggregate. **We do not sell your personal information.**

## AI processing

To generate responses, your questions and background are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic). Some advisors may run web searches based on your question. Providers process this data under their own terms and policies.

## Sharing

We share data only with providers that help us run the service (hosting, AI, payments, email), when required by law, or when you create a public share link.

## Retention and your choices

We keep your data while your account exists. In **Settings** you can download all your data, turn off reminder emails, or delete your account, which permanently removes your meetings, plans and share links. Backups expire on their normal schedule.

## Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2, session and reset tokens are stored only as hashes, and all traffic should be served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

## Children

{product} is not intended for anyone under 18.
